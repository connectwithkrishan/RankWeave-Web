Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about RankWeave? Find quick answers for Buyers, Site Owners, and Agencies below.

General Questions

Q: What is RankWeave?

RankWeave is a global digital marketplace connecting buyers (SEO professionals, marketers, and business owners) directly with verified website owners, outreach partners, and digital agencies for high-quality guest posts, link insertions, and digital services—without middleman markups.

Q: Is RankWeave free to use?

Yes! Browsing listings, searching by niche, applying filters, and messaging sellers is 100% free for buyers. For website owners and agencies, listing your domains starts with a free explorer tier and affordable listing passes for verified badges.

Q: How does RankWeave prevent spam and fake domains?

We enforce strict domain and profile verification protocols. Website owners must verify domain access via DNS TXT records, HTML meta tags, or admin proof. Every listing is audited for Domain Authority (DA), Page Authority (PA), Organic Traffic, and Spam Score to eliminate PBNs and fake metric claims.

For Buyers & SEO Agencies

Q: How do I buy a guest post or link insertion?

  • 1. Search & Filter: Use our marketplace filters to find websites by Niche, DA/DR, Organic Traffic, and Country.
  • 2. Direct Chat: Message the seller directly in real-time to discuss your content requirements, anchor text, turnaround time (TAT), and pricing.
  • 3. Confirm & Publish: Finalize the deal terms directly with the seller and get your live link publication.

Q: What are Verified Badges (Green vs. Blue Checks)?

  • Green Verified Check: Awarded to Verified Users (Individual Buyers, Direct Website Owners, Freelancers, and Independent Outreach Specialists) who have completed their account identity and profile verification.
  • Blue Verified Check: Awarded to Verified Companies & Agencies (Digital Marketing Agencies, SEO Firms, Corporate Publishers, and Registered Business Entities) after vetting their corporate credentials and service portfolios.

Q: Do you guarantee search engine ranking improvements?

While RankWeave guarantees real-time verified domain metrics and authentic site connections, search engine algorithms depend on multiple external factors. We do not guarantee specific ranking movements.

For Website Owners & Outreach Editors

Q: How do I list my website on RankWeave?

Simply create an account on the RankWeave Web Dashboard or Mobile App, click "Add New Listing", enter your domain URL, select your niche, set your turnaround time (TAT), and set your desired prices for Guest Posts or Link Insertions.

Q: How do I get a Verified Badge for my listings?

  • To get a Green Check (Verified User), complete the profile verification check inside your seller dashboard.
  • To get a Blue Check (Verified Company/Agency), register as an Agency/Company profile and submit corporate verification credentials during setup.

Q: Can I set my own prices for my listings?

Yes, absolutely! As a seller or outreach partner, you have 100% control over your pricing, content guidelines, and turnaround times.

Platform Security & Account

Q: How do I download the RankWeave Mobile App?

You can download our official mobile app directly from the Google Play Store for Android or access our Web Dashboard at app.rankweaveapp.com from any web browser.

Q: How do I delete my account or listings?

You can delete your account at any time directly inside the app under Profile -> Delete Account or by visiting our dedicated Account Deletion page (/delete-account). All your stored data will be permanently removed within 30 days.
